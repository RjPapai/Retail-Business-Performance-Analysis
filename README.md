# 🛍️ Retail Business Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail business sales dataset using Python. The objective is to understand customer behavior, sales performance, product profitability, regional performance, and generate actionable business insights through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
- Analyze sales and profit performance
- Discover business insights
- Identify top-performing products and regions
- Provide business recommendations
- Visualize important KPIs

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Source:** Superstore Sales Dataset

Dataset includes:

- Order Date
- Customer Information
- Product Details
- Region
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology category generated the highest sales.
- West region achieved the highest sales.
- Consumer segment contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Higher discounts often reduced profitability.
- Technology products were the most profitable.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Focus more on Technology products.
- Reduce unnecessary discount strategies.
- Improve Furniture category profitability.
- Increase marketing in low-performing regions.
- Promote high-performing products.
