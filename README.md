# 📊 Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

A complete Data Analytics project developed to analyze retail business performance using **Python, SQL (SQLite), and Power BI**. The project focuses on sales trends, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, and product insights to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset to identify key business insights through:

- Data Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- SQL Business Analysis
- Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- Business Recommendations

The objective is to demonstrate an end-to-end Business Analytics workflow, from raw data to actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the most profitable product categories.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Evaluate customer segments.
- Measure the impact of discounts on profit.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest profit.
- Furniture produced comparatively lower profit margins.
- The West region achieved the strongest sales performance.
- Higher discounts negatively impacted profitability.
- A small number of products contributed significantly to total sales.
- Consumer segment generated the highest revenue.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in Technology products.
- Reduce excessive discount strategies.
- Improve Furniture profitability.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing products.
- Improve performance in lower-performing regions.
- Continue strengthening the Consumer segment.
